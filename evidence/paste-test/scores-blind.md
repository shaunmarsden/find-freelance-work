Judgement calls, fixed before unblinding:

- **Fennick Home.** Every reply said SKIP. Most added that they could counter-offer at about £200, or that it was worth a look only if the client paid more. That is a change in the client's price, not the freelancer relaxing their minimum, so I scored it right.
- **Hartley Joinery.** The pre-registration fixed APPLY as the only right answer. Every reply, including the APPLY ones, raised the same caution: a form that stops sending after a hosting move is usually an email-delivery problem, which the freelancer hasn't fixed before. Two replies called it MAYBE for that reason. I scored them wrong as planned, but it is a defensible call.
- **The £20 fee.** All nine spreadsheet replies said SKIP. Eight told the freelancer not to pay; one (f339124) said to ignore it without mentioning the fee. None recommended paying.
