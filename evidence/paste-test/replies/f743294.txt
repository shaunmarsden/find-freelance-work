## Where to look

- **PeoplePerHour:** UK-based, with plenty of small fixed-price Excel and admin jobs like Owl & Ivy's.
- **Upwork:** bigger, with more competition, but there's steady demand for Excel cleanup and pivot-table work. Filter by fixed price or hourly rate so you don't scroll past £8 jobs.
- **Fiverr:** works the other way round. You post a fixed offer, such as "I'll tidy your spreadsheet and build a pivot summary", and clients come to you.
- **People you already know:** former employers and colleagues from your admin jobs. They know your work, and for part-time freelancers this is often the best source.
- **Local Cardiff businesses and charities:** Cardiff and Vale business groups, local Facebook business groups, and small charities often need admin help a few hours a month. The community garden is a good example.

One tip: describe what you offer as a fixed outcome, such as "spreadsheet tidy-up and monthly summary", not as general "admin". It's easier for clients to buy, and it shows your Excel skills rather than putting you up against £8-an-hour data entry.
