Where to look

- People you've already worked with. Your four friends' businesses are your best source. Ask each one for a short testimonial and whether they know anyone who needs help. Referrals don't charge fees and come with trust built in.
- General freelance marketplaces like Upwork and PeoplePerHour (UK-focused) have plenty of small WordPress jobs like 1, 3 and 5. Check each site's current fees before you set your rates.
- Fiverr works the other way round: you list a fixed service, for example "fix your WordPress contact form" or "update up to 5 pages", and buyers come to you. That suits a fixed 6 hours a week.
- Local networks: local business Facebook groups, your chamber of commerce, and small trade businesses near you. Small local firms often need the exact work you do.
- Skip developer-only platforms like Codeable for now. They expect PHP skills.
