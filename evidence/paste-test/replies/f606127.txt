**Places worth looking**

**Two practical points before you start**

- Check your part-time job contract for anything about second jobs or working for competitors.
- Freelance income goes through Self Assessment with HMRC. The first £1,000 a year of trading income is covered by the trading allowance. Above that, you'll need to register as self-employed.
