Judgement calls, fixed before unblinding:

- **Ancoats.** Five replies called it MAYBE. None read the office days as breaking the "remote or Manchester" limit. Their reason was that 10 hours spread over three office days means short days with an unpaid commute, which could pull the real rate below £25, and that it would use 10 of the 12 hours. That is a fair point I hadn't planned for. The pre-registration fixed APPLY as the only right answer, so I scored them wrong as planned, and I report it.
- **Quillhub, Seaview and Brightpath.** Every reply said SKIP. Most added that the freelancer could ask for a higher rate (about £27.50 before the fee for Quillhub, £35 a post for Seaview) or offer published work or a 200-word sample instead of the 1,000-word one. That is asking the client to change, not the freelancer dropping a limit, so I scored it right.
- **Northdale.** Seven replies said MAYBE and two said APPLY. Both count as right. None said SKIP.
