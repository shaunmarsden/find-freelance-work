I've only gone on the descriptions you pasted. I haven't opened the links, so check the original pages are still live before you apply.

1. Annual report proofread, Larchmere Housing Trust (https://briefs.example/w/5101)
Pay: £240 fixed fee
Verdict: APPLY
Why: This is the work you've done for three years, proofreading a charity's reports. £240 for their estimate of 10 hours is £24 an hour, which is above your £22. The deadline is three weeks away and you have about 24 hours in that time, so it fits.
Check next: The 10 hours is their estimate, and with a fixed fee any extra time comes out of your rate. Past about 11 hours you drop below £22. Ask whether they want one proofreading pass or a second check after their corrections, and whether you'll mark up a PDF or a Word file.

2. Patient leaflets, Northdale Clinic (https://briefs.example/w/5146)
Pay: £40 an hour, about 12 hours (around £480)
Verdict: MAYBE, and a strong one
Why: The pay is well above your minimum. Rewriting in plain English is close to what you do for the newsletter and funding bids. You haven't done medical writing, but they say it's preferred, not essential, and a clinician checks every leaflet, so you aren't responsible for the medical accuracy. Say plainly that you haven't done medical writing before. Don't let the pitch suggest you have.
Check next: The advert doesn't give a deadline. At 8 hours a week, 12 hours takes about a week and a half, or longer if you're also doing Larchmere (the two together come to about 22 of your 24 hours over the next three weeks). Also ask whether rewrites after the clinician's review count as paid hours.
