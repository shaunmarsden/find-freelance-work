# Worked example: fictional freelance project review

**This is entirely fictional and illustrates how to use the skill.** No live job search was conducted and no client, budget or opportunity below is real.

**User:** “I can edit WordPress sites and automate basic spreadsheets. I have 6 hours a week and prefer remote work. Is this project worth applying for?”

**Fictional brief supplied by the user:**

> “Fix a WordPress contact form and update three website pages. Fixed budget £180. Must be completed within five working days. Need reliable communication and examples of similar work.”

**AI skill response (illustrative):**

**MAYBE — reasonable fit, but clarify the deadline and access.**

- **Why it might fit:** Editing pages and troubleshooting a standard contact form are within your stated capabilities. £180 could be reasonable for a very small project.
- **What's uncertain:** You haven't confirmed experience fixing the particular form plugin; the advert gives no estimate of current site problems. Five working days may be tight at six available hours weekly.
- **Before applying:** Ask which form/plugin is affected, what “fix” means and whether staging access is available. Estimate hours before judging the budget.
- **Suggested reply (only if you can deliver the work):** “I can help with WordPress page edits and contact-form troubleshooting. Which form plugin is involved, and what happens when someone submits the form? That will help me assess the scope and timing.”
- **Source status:** Supplied fictional brief; there is no real listing or verifiable application link.

**Another fictional scenario:** The advert says “Build a complex Shopify marketplace integration in 24 hours” but the freelancer has only used basic WordPress editing. **SKIP** because the required expertise and timeframe are unsupported.

**What this example demonstrates:** Clear decisions grounded in supplied facts, honest unknowns and a practical next question. It does not demonstrate successful freelance discovery or earnings.
